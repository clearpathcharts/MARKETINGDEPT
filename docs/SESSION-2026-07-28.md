## Who / what

Clear Path Market Science — [clearpathtrader.com](https://clearpathtrader.com).  
Mission: market education for wounded veterans and neurodivergent learners without ticker flash that can trigger PTSD or seizures. Education / analytics only — not a brokerage.

MYEYES OS was built in Google AI Studio as a Zapier-like private corporate control plane. Audit found almost all “Connected” / KPI / telemetry UI was mock data.

## Decisions

1. Prefer honest status over fake telemetry.
2. Discovery ranking focuses on helping users **find** ClearPath (search, calm education, community, AI citations, audio) — not generic Zapier popularity.
3. Private Buffer-style **Publishing** for ClearPath education posts (YouTube / Telegram / Discord first).
4. Live social posts still require explicit confirmation; Zapier MCP is the Phase 1 send path.
5. Backup target: `clearpathcharts/MARKETINGDEPT`.
